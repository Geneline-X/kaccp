# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in KACCP a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Where This Applies

This Code of Conduct governs **both**:

1. **The open-source project** — issues, pull requests, discussions and any other contributor space.
2. **The KACCP platform itself** — including written review feedback exchanged between reviewers,
   transcribers and speakers.

The second matters as much as the first. Review feedback is the main way people on the platform
communicate, and it carries a power imbalance: a reviewer decides whether someone's work is paid.
Feedback must explain what was wrong and how to fix it. It must never disparage the contributor.

## Our Standards

**Positive behaviour includes:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

**Unacceptable behaviour includes:**
- Harassment, trolling, or insulting comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- **Demeaning review feedback** — rejecting work with insults, mockery, or comments about the
  person rather than the work
- **Retaliatory rejection** — rejecting valid work because of a disagreement or complaint

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers at
**info@geneline-x.net**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a
response appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality
regarding reporters.

**On the platform**, administrators can deactivate an account immediately, which revokes access
while preserving records needed for any investigation. Contributors who believe a moderation or
review decision was unfair may appeal to the same address.

**Contributors must be 18 or older** — see the [Terms of Service](TERMS.md). Minors are not
permitted to contribute, so this Code of Conduct assumes an adult user base. Any account found to
belong to someone under 18 is deactivated and their unpublished recordings deleted.
